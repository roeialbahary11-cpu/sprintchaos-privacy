SprintChaos

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

Publisher: Roei Albahary (ALBA), Israel

SprintChaos uses online services to authenticate players, save game progress, run multiplayer races, maintain rankings, and support friend connections. SprintChaos does not sell personal information and does not use your data for cross-app tracking or third-party advertising.

Information We Process

  • Account information, including a Unity Authentication player ID, player display name, and identifiers for linked sign-in providers such as Apple Game Center or a Unity Player Account. If a player chooses to use a Unity Player Account, Unity may also collect the player's email address for account access.
  • Social and multiplayer information, including friend relationships, lobby and session participation, and player IDs needed to connect players and display race participants.
  • Gameplay information, including progress, virtual-currency balances, owned cosmetic items, mission progress, rank, leaderboard entries, and race results.
  • Technical information needed to operate and secure online services, such as IP address and the coarse location it may indicate, device and platform information, app version, connection information, and crash, performance, and diagnostic data.
  • Customer-support information generated if a player contacts support through a Unity Player Account.

Personal profile photos are disabled in the current release. SprintChaos does not upload or store personal profile photos while this feature is disabled.

How We Use Information

We process this information only as needed to:

  • create and recover player accounts;
  • synchronize progress and virtual items across sessions;
  • operate multiplayer races, rankings, missions, and friend features;
  • detect abuse, prevent fraud, and protect game integrity;
  • understand feature usage and improve game reliability; and
  • maintain the security, reliability, and performance of the game.

Service Providers

SprintChaos uses Unity Gaming Services for authentication, cloud save, cloud code, multiplayer connectivity, friends, and leaderboards. Apple Game Center may provide platform sign-in and gaming services on iOS. These providers process information under their own privacy terms and may process data in countries other than your own.

Sharing, Advertising, and Tracking

We share information with service providers only as necessary to operate the features described above, comply with law, or protect users and the service. SprintChaos does not sell your personal information, does not display third-party advertising, and does not use your data for cross-app tracking.

Data Retention and Account Deletion

Game information is retained while an account is active and for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the game, maintain security, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations.

You can permanently delete your SprintChaos account from Settings > Account > Delete Account. The deletion process removes saved SprintChaos game data and friend relationships, deletes the Unity Authentication player account, and clears local game data. Deletion is permanent. Deleting a SprintChaos account does not delete an Apple, Google, or Unity-wide account that may also be used by other apps.

Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information. Use the in-game account deletion feature or contact us using the address below. We may need to verify a request before completing it.

Children

SprintChaos is currently rated for users age 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. If the minimum digital-consent age in your country is higher, use of online account features should be supervised or authorized by a parent or guardian as required by local law.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect game information. No online service or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the game, its online services, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page shows when the policy was last updated.
